# Make Stargate yours

Choose a look you enjoy. Your theme does not change your access or where traffic goes.

[Back to README](../README.md) · [Full user guide](user-guide.md)

## Change the theme

1. Open Stargate from the browser's Extensions menu.
2. Choose **Settings** to open the full settings page.
3. Under **Appearance**, choose a theme:

| Theme | Look |
| --- | --- |
| **Clear** | Light surfaces and blue accents |
| **Stargate** | A dark blue interface and portal artwork; the default |
| **Observatory** | A purple interface with a ringed planet |

The choice is saved in this browser profile. The same controls and routes remain
available in every theme. Theme assets are local; choosing one downloads nothing.

## Choose less motion

In **Settings → Appearance → Motion**, choose **Reduce motion** if you prefer
still effects. **Follow my device** uses your device's motion preference.
Every feature works without animation, and toolbar indicators are static.

## Keep advanced mode off until you need it

**Advanced mode** shows extra controls; switching the mode on does not itself
change routes. Most people can leave it off.

The extra controls include:

- **Direct local-network access:** off by default. Enabling it deliberately lets
  matching private-IP requests go directly from your browser, outside Stargate.
- **IPFS gateway:** choose a compatible HTTPS gateway for new IPFS opens. Existing
  tabs keep their current addresses.
- **Saved device access:** inspect, replace or forget this browser's saved access.
  Forgetting it locally does not revoke it on the server; ask the operator to
  revoke a lost or exposed device.

Read the [advanced-setting details](user-guide.md#appearance-and-advanced-settings)
before changing these options. For IPFS address-bar links, follow the separate
[browser setup steps](user-guide.md#optional-open-ipfs-addresses).
